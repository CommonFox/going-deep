Two hard rules

Compare POR. Take the bigger number.

A Tier-2 WR does outscore a Tier-3 RB — but that choice never reaches this seat. At every one of your picks the RBs on the board sit a full tier above the WRs. It only flips in round 5+ (RB T5 ~43 vs a fallen WR T5 ~55).

fade Unproven-Veteran WRs

Never-elite, 27+ or 7+ years: Christian Watson, Jakobi Meyers, Michael Pittman. −3.8 pts under ADP over 8 seasons — the one reliable negative archetype signal. Take an Unproven-Youth WR instead.

Young or in-prime and already has an elite finish. 52% elite-finish rate, 5% bust, and the only reliably positive archetype. Gibbs, Bijan, Achane, Jeanty, Chase Brown, Breece Hall, Kyren Williams, Bucky Irving, Javonte Williams.

Tiebreakers

Round 4–5 RB → Bucky Irving (Proven-Prime + ~91% available).   TE → McBride, else Bowers (board favors McBride by 14 POR).   QB: QBs never bust — 0% across every archetype. They get hurt or they deliver. That's why 2-QB is safe, not just high-EV.

Endgame notes

The value window at this seat is rounds 6–9 (picks 84, 85, 112, 113), not 12–15. By round 12 every player realistically available has negative POR — picks 168/169 and 196/197 are dart-throws plus K and DST.

In-house model vs market — higher than consensus: Jadarian Price (RB, buried Lions backup the model likes anyway), Sam LaPorta (TE), Harold Fannin (TE). Lower — fade as depth: Rico Dowdle (RB).

TE patience: the McBride + Bowers tier is worth ~20–35 POR more than Tyler Warren. Miss both → no urgency, Warren is ~76% at pick 84. Don't reach for TE3 in rounds 5–6.

Splitting the QBs (one at 28, one at 56) gambles on Jordan Love at ~66% and nothing viable behind him. If you want them separate, still take both across 28 & 29.
